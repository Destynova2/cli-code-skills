# Managing and Negotiating Priorities

Use for corrective feedback, delegation, coaching, peer influence, and
conflicts between teams. Nothing here is bargained: no anchors, no
concession plans, no Voss kit. Open questions and a fair summary are enough.
Priority arbitration with your own management is a negotiation: use
`cli-forge-nego` (`defend` mode).

## Pick the Conversation

| Stake | Method | Outcome sought |
|---|---|---|
| A specific behaviour caused a problem | SBI: situation, observable behaviour, impact. Then ask for intent and their account (SBII). | An expected behaviour and a moment to check the change. |
| A report needs help | *The Coaching Habit*: ask where they are stuck and what help would move them before prescribing. | An action they choose and concrete support from the manager. |
| Two teams clash | Describe the disagreement the way an observer both sides accept would (*Difficult Conversations*). | A shared interest, an explicit divergence, and a decision criterion. |
| You need a peer's backing | *Influence Without Authority*: understand their constraint, find a contribution that is genuinely useful within their mandate. | A reciprocal commitment they can own in front of their team. |
| You need to delegate | State the expected result, the decisions delegated, resources, check-in point, and the signal that justifies escalation. | A mandate understood, with real autonomy. |

## Lines to Adapt

**To a report.**

> « Mardi en revue, tu as annoncé la livraison avant que Léa ait fini ses
> tests. Elle a dû rappeler le client pour décaler. Comment tu avais compris
> la décision prise en réunion ? »

Listen before agreeing who gives the go-ahead. If the facts come from a third
party, say so and check them.

**Between peers.**

> « L'exploitation veut garder un déploiement qu'elle sait rétablir. La
> plateforme veut passer moins de temps sur les mises à jour. Quel exercice
> nous permettrait de comparer les deux options sur ces critères-là ? »

## Rules

- If the decision is imposed, say clearly what is decided and name what is
  still the person's choice. Do not dress an order as a negotiation.
- In a real disagreement, leave a genuine possibility of correction or refusal.
- If the user has no authority to promise budget or a priority change, write a
  recommendation to carry to the decision-maker, not a promise.
- HR consequences (warning, dismissal, performance plan): Formal register,
  facts and dates only, refer to HR or a legal adviser.
